Mode A

Univariate Report Studio

Answers 7 foundational questions for a single series (e.g. Monthly India Electricity Demand):

1 What does my data look like? (Stats, count, gaps)
2 What is it made of? (Trend, season & residual)
3 Is it stable or drifting? (ADF test, d & D recommendation)
4 How does it remember its past? (ACF/PACF Assistant)
5 Which model fits best? (Holdout RMSE Leaderboard)
6 Can I trust the model? (Residuals & Ljung-Box verdict)
7 What happens next? (95% uncertainty forecast & CSV)

Mode B

Multivariate VAR / VARMA Studio

Evaluates interconnected systems (Electricity, Temperature, Industrial Output, Renewables):

Do they move together? Correlation matrix & co-movement
Does A help predict B? Granger causality tests & verdicts
How long does a shock last? Impulse response curves (IRF)
What drives the uncertainty? Variance decomposition (FEVD)
Joint Forecasts: Multi-step paths for each series with 95% bands
