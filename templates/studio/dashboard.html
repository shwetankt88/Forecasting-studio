Univariate Forecasting Suite

Automated single-metric modeling answering 7 fundamental analytical questions for reliable capacity planning and demand projections:

1 Data Inspection: Timeline trajectory, summary statistics, and gap detection.
2 Decomposition: Extraction of macro trend, seasonal cycles, and irregular residuals.
3 Stationarity Audit: Augmented Dickey-Fuller tests determining differencing order ($d, D$).
4 Memory Structure: ACF & PACF correlograms with automated pattern reading.
5 Model Leaderboard: Candidate grid (AR, MA, ARIMA, SARIMA) ranked by holdout RMSE.
6 Residual Diagnostics: Ljung-Box test verifying uncaptured autocorrelation.
7 Confidence Forecasts: Multi-step horizon projections with bounded 95% bands.

Multivariate System Dynamics (VAR)

Joint modeling for interconnected economic, energy, and climate variables to measure dynamic shock propagation:

Co-Movement & Correlation: Pearson correlation matrix and normalized time paths.
Granger Causality: Formal statistical testing to establish cross-variable predictive signals.
Impulse Response Functions (IRF): Orthogonal simulations of how shocks ripple through the system.
Variance Decomposition (FEVD): Quantifies which variables drive long-term uncertainty.
Joint Forecasts: Synchronized out-of-sample paths with compound prediction bands.
